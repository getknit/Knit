A LoRa radio that stopped sending, and a new group's first message that never arrived.

Messages sent from several places at once could trip the queue that feeds the radio. Everything behind it
then stayed unsent while the radio still showed as connected, and inbound messages kept arriving, so the
only sign was that nothing you sent was getting through. Reopening the app cleared it until next time.
Only phones with a Meshtastic radio paired were affected; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth carried messages normally
throughout.

The first message in a new group now reaches every member. Knit sent the key for that message a moment
before it sent the group itself, so on a member's phone the key had nothing to attach to and was dropped;
the group then showed up with no message in it, and the sender never saw a tick. The receiving phone now
keeps that key until the group arrives, and the message opens as soon as it does.
